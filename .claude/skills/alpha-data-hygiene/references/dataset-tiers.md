# Live-able vs research-only

The uncomfortable pattern: the datasets with the best alpha prospects are exactly the ones
that cannot be obtained live at student cost, and the live-able ones are the crowded ones.

## Live-able — a signal built here can become a strategy
| Dataset | How, live |
|---|---|
| Prices / volume | Polygon, IEX, any broker — cheap or free |
| Fundamentals | SEC EDGAR real-time, free |
| Short interest | FINRA direct, free, twice monthly |
| Fama-French factors | Ken French site, free |
| 13F ownership | EDGAR free, but **45 days after quarter-end by law** — a permanent lag that applies equally to everyone, which is what makes it a candidate for a *structural* edge rather than an informational one |

## Research-only — anything built on these is a PAPER, not a strategy
Revere supply chain, RavenPack news, Panjiva shipping, IBES estimates, OptionMetrics, TAQ,
Markit CDS, MSCI ESG, and CRSP itself (20 months stale).

**If a hypothesis needs any of these, stop and say so before building it.**
